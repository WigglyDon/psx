# psx identity

One blue Cross on a black circular button, set into warm grey plastic. The
owner requested a close-up of the original controller era's Cross button,
replacing the generic controller silhouette. The vectors are drawn for psx
and use the project's MIT license. Use lowercase **psx** in copy. Machine
Rhythm is the maker.

`mark.svg` is the transparent button master. `icon.svg` adds the grey plastic
tile and can serve as an SVG favicon. `favicon.ico` contains 16, 32 and 48 pixel
tiles; `icon-512.png` is the raster export. `readme.svg` is the repository
masthead. Subtle cap and plastic shading suggest the physical button; the
Cross stays a solid fill so it reads at small sizes.

Android's adaptive foreground uses the same geometry at 74% scale, keeping
the entire button within the 66-point safe circle. Its background is flat grey
so launcher masks and motion have no exposed seams. The themed monochrome
variant cuts the Cross out of the button silhouette; Android supplies its
palette. The blue/grey appearance applies to the ordinary full-color icon.

Colors: plastic `#B9B9B2`, cap `#141619`–`#2D3034`, Cross `#7B9DCF`.
The masthead retains the dark product background and outlined psx wordmark.
A favicon asset does not imply that the repository has a hosted website.

PlayStation names and the requested button reference identify the emulated
system. Sony branding is not owned by this project; MIT covers our drawn
artwork, not third-party trademark rights. The identity contains no Sony logo
or startup artwork and implies no affiliation. Store branding review remains
part of release preparation.
